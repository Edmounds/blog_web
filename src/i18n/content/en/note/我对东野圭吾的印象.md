# My Impressions of Keigo Higashino
	I believe the first work by Keigo Higashino I read was *The Miracles of the Namiya General Store*.
	I remember it was when I was in elementary school. I was spending my summer vacation at my cousin's house, and since there were no phones to play with back then, I could only read. As luck would have it, there was a copy of *The Miracles of the Namiya General Store*. Judging solely from the title, I thought it was the kind of book meant for casual, everyday reading. It turned out to be quite compelling—the redemption achieved through letters that crossed time and space also made me remember the author.
	The next time I encountered his work was in my second year of high school.
	At the time, people in our dormitory began passing around *Journey Under the Midnight Sun*. I remember at least three people finished *Journey Under the Midnight Sun* on that Kindle. We were truly neglecting sleep and meals over it—it was almost as intense as binge-watching *Breaking Bad*. BTW, even when I follow web novels, I've never been as enthusiastic as I was with this. When it was finally my turn, I would basically hurry to finish my homework during self-study period and then secretly start reading, read while eating, and keep reading even after the dormitory lights-out time (thanks to this backlit Kindle).
