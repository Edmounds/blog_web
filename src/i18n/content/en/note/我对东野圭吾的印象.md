# My impression of Keigo Higashino
The first time I read Keigo Higashino's work was probably his "The Worry-Free Grocery Store"

I remember that I was in elementary school, and I was playing at my cousin's house during the summer vacation. There were no mobile phones to play with, so I had to read books. There happened to be a "Jieyou Grocery Store". From the title alone, I thought it was a daily leisure book. This book is quite exciting. The redemption achieved through letters across time and space also made me remember this author.
The next time we see each other will be our sophomore year of high school.

At that time, "White Night Journey" began to be circulated in our dormitory. I remember that at least three people watched "White Night Journey" on that kindle. At that time, I really forgot all about sleep and food. Compared with chasing "Breaking Bad", btw I am not as active in chasing online novels as I am chasing this one. When it was my turn to read, I basically just finished my homework in self-study class and started watching secretly, reading while eating, and continued reading even when the lights were turned off in the dormitory (thanks to this kindle with backlight). This was the first time I read such a suspense novel that was a bit large in scale. Although it was dark, it had a sense of reality, and the main plot of the male and female perspective was also quite interesting. More importantly, the plot was really full of ups and downs, and the murderer was difficult to guess. The most disappointing thing is that Kirihara Ryoji finally committed suicide to protect Yukiho, and Yukiho was able to say "I don't know this person at all" with an expressionless face.

The next time I saw "Xue Sui" was in "Fantasy Night". This story was not as exciting as "White Night Journey", so it was not satisfactory. The ending of "Fantasy Night" is also very similar to "White Night Walk". The male protagonist finally knows that the female protagonist has been deceiving and using him, but in the end he commits suicide to protect the female protagonist and let the female protagonist escape innocently.

I won’t go into details about the other books one by one. The pictures below are the works of Keigo Higashino that I have read.![Works of Keigo Higashino I have read|475](https://img.muelsyse.us/bed/20260808043442545.png)


Keigo Higashino is a great writer. Thanks to him for allowing me, who is not used to reading, to concentrate on reading a complete book. Thanks to him for creating stories that are endlessly memorable.

R.I.P.
