## About This Website
This is my quiet personal space—for writing articles, taking notes, sharing projects, and keeping a record of the books and films I have experienced.
This is not social media. There are no notifications or anxiety over likes, only words, photos, and a few records.
I dislike the intense influence and intrusion that social media apps have on personal life.
