## About the Website
This is my quiet personal space—to write articles, take notes, share projects, and record the books and movies I have read and watched.
This is not social media. There are no notifications or anxiety over likes, only words, photos, and a few records.
I don't like the intense influence and intrusion that social media apps have on personal life.
