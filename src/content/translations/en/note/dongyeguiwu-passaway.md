# My Impression of Keigo Higashino
	I think the first work by Keigo Higashino I encountered was *The Miracles of the Namiya General Store*.
	I remember it was during elementary school. I was spending my summer vacation at my cousin's house, and back then, without a phone or tablet to play with, I could only read books, so that was how I was introduced to Keigo Higashino.
